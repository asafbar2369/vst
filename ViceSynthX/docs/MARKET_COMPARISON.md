## Positioning of VICE SYNTH X (engineering view, not marketing)
- Comparable scope in oscillator types to Serum 2 (wavetable, analog, sample, multisample/SFZ, granular, spectral), but with far
  smaller content (85 presets / 27 wavetables vs. 626 / 288 stated for Serum 2) and no MPE.
- Differentiators that exist in code and are tested: a conversational AI designer with six provider modes including fully
  offline operation and Hebrew input; validated patch transactions with undo and A/B; reference matching that produces
  editable presets. Synplant 2 (local, AI-based Genopatch) and Massive XL (Preset Generator) offer audio-to-patch workflows;
  no matched-condition comparison has been made, so no superiority claim is possible.
- Gaps against most competitors: MPE, layer architecture, effects count, library size, modulation sources beyond the
  current 4 envelopes / 10 LFOs / 8 macros / 32 slots. See FEATURE_GAP_ANALYSIS.md.
